# Briefing Note: U.S. Nonresident Enrollment, Fall 2024

**Audience:** research, policy, and communications leadership  
**Coverage:** degree-granting institutions in the IPEDS First Look/Digest Title IV universe, 50 states + DC, Fall 2015-2024  
**Latest data status:** Provisional

## Executive summary

Fall 2024 U.S. nonresident enrollment reached **1,044,137 students**, or **5.31%** of total enrollment in the analytic universe. That is **+29,539 students (+2.9%)** from 2023.

The newest annual movement differs from the longer-run pattern. From 2023 to 2024, undergraduate U.S. nonresident enrollment changed by **+31,592**, while graduate enrollment changed by **-2,053**. Across the full 2015-2024 period, graduate enrollment changed by **+138,765** and undergraduate enrollment by **-75,365**, for a net national change of **+63,400**.

The latest increase is primarily movement at institutions already in the analytic universe. Continuing institutions account for **+29,968** of the 2023-2024 change, while entry and exit from the analytic universe net to **-429**.

## What merits attention

- **Short-run composition changed.** The 2023-2024 increase is undergraduate-led even though the decade-level increase is graduate-led. One year is not enough to establish a new trend, so the next release should test whether this shift persists.
- **State movement is uneven.** FL posted the largest latest-year increase (**+6,207**), while MA posted the largest decline (**-1,437**). Across the post-pandemic comparison, **33** states/DC are at or above their 2019 level and **18** remain below it.
- **Institution-level QA still matters.** The latest-release review contains **27 high-priority** and **50 medium-priority** records for analyst review. These flags identify changes that warrant investigation; they are not automatic findings that the source data are wrong.
- **The national increase is not an artifact of a larger institution universe.** Across 2015-2024, 3,470 continuing institutions contribute **+66,981**, while universe entry/exit nets to **-3,581**.

## Questions for the next update

1. Does the undergraduate-led movement observed in 2024 persist in the next Fall Enrollment release, or revert toward the graduate-led pattern seen over 2015-2024?
2. Are the largest state changes broad-based across institutions, or concentrated in a small number of campuses? The current QA queue should be resolved before institution-specific changes are elevated into public narrative.
3. Do complementary population-specific sources show similar or different patterns? IPEDS U.S. nonresident status should not be used as a proxy for undocumented, immigrant-origin, DACA, or foreign-born populations.
4. Have revised IPEDS files or a newer NCES Digest changed any benchmark values or release-status language?

## Interpretation boundaries

This analysis supports enrollment monitoring, not causal claims about immigration policy. IPEDS U.S. nonresident status does not identify immigration status or country of origin, and Fall Enrollment is a point-in-time snapshot. State totals describe institution location. Institution structures, UNITIDs, eligibility, and reporting practices can change over time.
