# Latest Release Review

This review compares Fall 2023 with Fall 2024 (provisional). It is generated by `scripts/build_update_review.py` from the normalized IPEDS institution-year data.

A review flag is a **triage signal, not a finding that the source is wrong**. Large changes can reflect real enrollment movement, reporting changes, mergers/reorganizations, classification changes, or data-quality issues that require source review.

## National update

U.S. nonresident enrollment increased from **1,014,598** to **1,044,137**, a change of **+29,539 (+2.9%)**.

The composition of the newest increase differs from the decade-level pattern: undergraduate U.S. nonresident enrollment changed by **+31,592**, while graduate enrollment changed by **-2,053**. The two components reconcile exactly to the national change.

## Institution accounting

- **57** institutions left the analytic universe after reporting **592** U.S. nonresident students in 2023.
- Universe entry/exit therefore nets to **-429**.

## QA triage queue

High priority is assigned for an absolute change of at least 1,000 students, a change of at least 100% from a baseline of 100 or more, or a zero/nonzero discontinuity that conflicts with recent history. Medium and low priorities use progressively smaller thresholds. Nonstandard imputation/status transitions and analytic-universe entry/exit are also surfaced.
