Interpretation rule

Evidence can clear a narrative claim without rewriting the source data.

Safe with context

External evidence supports the direction, approximate scale, or structural event strongly enough for bounded discussion.

Hold for confirmation

The IPEDS value remains in aggregates, but the institution-specific movement should not be explained as substantive change without a directly comparable source.

Scope control

International, F-1, SEVIS, and institution-defined global counts may use different populations or dates from IPEDS U.S. nonresident enrollment.

Internal composition check

The companion category diagnostic measures opposite movement inside the IPEDS race/ethnicity identity. It prioritizes review but cannot change a publication decision by itself.
