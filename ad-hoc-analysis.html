A01PASS

Simulated ad hoc request

Which states/DC are still below their 2019 U.S. nonresident enrollment, and how concentrated is the remaining shortfall?

Decision use
Prioritize where a follow-up geography review would start.
Answer
18 states/DC remain below 2019. Combined shortfall is -56,517 students. The five largest shortfalls (CA -33,282, OR -4,265, KS -3,842, WA -3,834, ID -3,648) account for 86.5% of it.
Authority
data/processed/state_change_summary.csv
QA
Below-baseline rows=18; shortfall sum=-56,517; top-five share=86.5%
A02PASS

Simulated ad hoc request

Which states contributed most to the 2023-2024 national increase?

Decision use
Identify the geographies driving the newest annual movement.
Answer
The five largest state gains contributed +17,753 students, or 60.1% of the +29,539 national gain. Largest gains: FL +6,207, KY +4,615, IN +2,422, TX +2,363, IL +2,146.
Authority
data/processed/state_year.csv; data/processed/national_year.csv
QA
All 51 state/DC changes sum to the national change: +29,539 = +29,539
A03PASS

Simulated ad hoc request

Which institutional-control sector explains the 2015-2024 net increase?

Decision use
Give management a concise structural explanation for the decade result.
Answer
Private nonprofit institutions increased by 82,825 students. That gain was partly offset by Private for-profit -3,338 and Public -16,087, reconciling to the national +63,400 change.
Authority
data/processed/control_year.csv; data/processed/national_year.csv
QA
Control-sector changes sum to national decade change: +63,400 = +63,400

Working pattern

A repeatable path for one-off analysis

1. Clarify

Confirm the population, geography, comparison period, and decision question before calculating.

2. Select authority

Use the smallest validated table that directly answers the request instead of rebuilding the entire project.

3. Reconcile

Check state sums, control-sector sums, or concentration arithmetic against an independent total.

4. Deliver

Return the answer first, then the small amount of evidence needed for the reader to trust and reuse it correctly.
