Interpret carefully

What this profile does and does not measure

Definition

IPEDS U.S. nonresident is a reporting category for students in the United States on a visa or temporary basis. It is not a complete measure of immigrant-origin, undocumented, DACA, or foreign-born students.

Geography

State assignment is based on institution location, not student residence, nationality, or immigration status.

Comparability

Institution eligibility, classifications, reporting practices, mergers, and UNITID structures can change. The project documents the common analytic universe and validates annual aggregates.
