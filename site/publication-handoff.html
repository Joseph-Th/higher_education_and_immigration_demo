Publication gate

Five questions before copy moves to a public page

The register makes the checks explicit so a source refresh cannot silently change population meaning, release status, arithmetic, or claim scope.

  1. DefinitionDoes the population and denominator still mean what the copy says?
  2. ReleaseIs the newest source final, provisional, revised, or from a different publication vintage?
  3. ReconcileDo components, states, and endpoint accounting reproduce the headline?
  4. CitationIs the maintained source appropriate as input, benchmark, methodology, or context?
  5. ScopeDoes unresolved QA require context or a claim-level hold before publication?
