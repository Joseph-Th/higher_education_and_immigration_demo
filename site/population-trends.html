R04 · bounded national comparison

Three population measures should not become one enrollment series.

IPEDS U.S. nonresident, immigrant-origin, and undocumented-student estimates answer different questions. This comparison keeps their definitions and source vintages visible, permits trend arithmetic only inside compatible segments, and uses same-year values only for scale context.

IPEDS

Institution-reported temporary-status U.S. nonresident category. It does not identify undocumented or DACA students as this category.

Immigrant-origin

Domestic first- and second-generation survey estimate. Nonimmigrant international students studying temporarily in the U.S. are excluded.

Undocumented

Modeled legal-status estimate from ACS microdata. It is conceptually part of the broader first-generation immigrant-origin population, not an independent add-on.

Geography

This note stays national. IPEDS uses institution location; CPS/ACS population estimates generally use residence, so state arithmetic is held.
