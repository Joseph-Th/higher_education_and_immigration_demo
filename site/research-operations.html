Shared lifecycle

Seven controls from partner kickoff to archive

The workflow keeps schedule assumptions, source files, methodology changes, discrepancies, publication decisions, and final handoff explicit.

  1. KickoffConfirm roles, scope, milestones, population, and deliverables.
  2. IntakeVersion the data, codebook/model notes, change log, and table shells.
  3. Method reviewCompare definitions, source vintage, weighting/model rules, and exclusions.
  4. ReproduceRebuild headline totals and reconcile components before interpretation.
  5. ResolveTrack partner questions and unexplained breaks to documented closure.
  6. PublishGate tables, methodology, Portal updates, and citations on QA/sign-off.
  7. ArchivePreserve final inputs, review log, approvals, and publication URL.

Handoff principle

Institutional knowledge is part of the deliverable

What gets archived

Source package, codebook/model notes, methodology version, reviewed tables, discrepancy log, approval record, and publication URL.

What never gets assumed

A fixed deadline, unchanged partner role, unchanged methodology, or direct comparability with a prior population/source merely because the report title is similar.

What blocks publication

Unreconciled headline totals, unexplained definition changes, missing source lineage, unresolved material discrepancies, or absent review/sign-off ownership.

What starts the next cycle

A known prior package, explicit open questions, current source metadata, and a schedule confirmed with the actual partner rather than inferred from history.
