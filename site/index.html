Important definition

U.S. nonresident is an IPEDS reporting category for students who are not U.S. citizens or nationals and are in the United States on a visa or temporary basis. It is not a measure of all immigrant, undocumented, DACA, or immigrant-origin students.

Finding 05

The decade’s growth shifted toward private nonprofit institutions

Private nonprofit institutions added U.S. nonresident students from 2015 to 2024 while public and private for-profit institutions declined overall. The nonprofit increase was overwhelmingly graduate enrollment.

Finding 06

The increase came from continuing institutions, not a bigger universe

Matching institutions by IPEDS UNITID shows that the decade-level gain survives changes in the set of institutions included in the analytic universe. Institutions present in both 2015 and 2024 account for more growth than the national net increase, while entry and exit from the universe net slightly negative.

Three flags, three different publication decisions

Methods & quality control

Built to be checked, not just viewed

The analysis keeps a source manifest, hashes every NCES archive, audits schema consistency across years, records imputation flags, and independently benchmarks the headline universe against an NCES Digest table.

—official NCES source archives logged with SHA-256 hashes

Analytic universe

Degree-granting institutions with PSET4FLG equal to 1 or 3, located in the 50 states and District of Columbia. NCES documents this derived flag as the selection used for First Look and Digest tables.

Measures

EFNRALT measures U.S. nonresident enrollment. EFTOTLT is total fall enrollment. EFALEVEL codes 1, 2, and 12 provide all-student, undergraduate, and graduate views.

Release handling

Revised historical files are preferred when NCES supplies an _rv file. Fall 2024 is labeled provisional. Source URLs, retrieval timestamps, archive sizes, and hashes are retained.

Limitations

Fall Enrollment is a point-in-time snapshot. Institution structures can change. State totals reflect institution location. U.S. nonresident status does not identify all immigrant-origin populations.

Population definitions

IPEDS, SEVIS, immigrant-origin, and undocumented estimates are not interchangeable

The project maintains a source-selection crosswalk and a bounded national comparison that documents who each dataset includes, what it excludes, the unit and vintage, and where trend arithmetic is methodologically defensible.

Release-vintage audit

Current files do not pretend to be an older publication

The March 2026 Digest used an earlier data vintage. The current IPEDS file was retrieved later, so subgroup counts differ slightly. The audit records those differences instead of overwriting current data to reproduce an older table.

What was checked before publication?
  • No duplicate selected institution-level rows.
  • All selected enrollment records join to the same-year institutional directory.
  • Enrollment counts are nonnegative and U.S. nonresident counts never exceed corresponding totals.
  • Required variables and aggregation labels are verified against every year’s official data dictionary.
  • Twenty-four metric-year consistency checks compare 2019–2024 results with NCES Digest 2025 Table 306.10.
  • Release-vintage differences in U.S. nonresident subgroup counts are quantified and retained rather than suppressed.

Reproduce it

Data, lineage, and audit outputs are public

The raw IPEDS archives are reproducibly downloaded from NCES and kept out of the repository. These compact outputs are enough to inspect the analysis and trace its source lineage.
