What changed

The latest undergraduate increase is unusually large and geographically broad.

Undergraduate enrollment rose in 45 states/DC, the widest breadth in the observed annual series. The national undergraduate increase is also the largest positive annual undergraduate movement since 2015. Those facts strengthen the baseline for monitoring, but they do not substitute for a second post-2024 observation.

Next-release rule

The baseline is fixed; the persistence judgment is still pending.

Do not overwrite the baseline

The incomplete candidate-year directory package is not used here. This baseline remains fixed at 2023-2024 even after the project later advances to a new data year.

Repeat the same measures

The next update should recompute national undergraduate/graduate change, state breadth, control decomposition, and institution continuity before comparing with this baseline.

Preserve schema review

A new release enters variable/code and universe review before any year-over-year persistence conclusion is published.

Keep interpretation bounded

A repeated enrollment pattern would establish persistence in the observed series, not a cause or immigration-policy effect.
