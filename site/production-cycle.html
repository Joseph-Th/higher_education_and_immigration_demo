Partner production cycle

Work advances on evidence, not on the calendar.

A stage-gated portfolio model for moving recurring partner research from confirmed kickoff through versioned intake, method review, QA, issue resolution, publication decision, and archival handoff.

Cycle register

Seven gates from kickoff to archive

Each stage has an entry condition, required record, explicit blocker, exit condition, and retained evidence. A stage cannot be completed merely because time passed.

Stage 1kickoff_scope_lock

Analyst + partner lead

Kickoff and scope lock

Entry
Confirmed production kickoff
Required record
Cycle brief with current partner contacts; roles; decision owner; milestones; source vintage; delivery format; planned outputs
Stop if
Missing decision owner; unconfirmed source vintage; inferred rather than confirmed milestones; undefined deliverables
Exit
Partner confirms scope; roles; milestones; source vintage; delivery format; and decision owner

Retain: Cycle brief and confirmed scope record

Stage 2versioned_intake

Analyst

Versioned intake

Entry
Kickoff scope is locked
Required record
Dated intake manifest; delivered source files; codebook or model notes; prior methodology; change log; table shells; uncertainty or suppression rules
Stop if
Missing or unreadable files; undocumented source; unversioned package; absent methodology needed to reproduce the product
Exit
Complete versioned package is preserved and every required input is either present or explicitly logged as missing

Retain: Intake manifest and preserved source package

Stage 3method_change_review

Analyst + partner method lead

Method-change review

Entry
Versioned intake is complete
Required record
Current-versus-prior comparison of population definition; source vintage; geography; denominators; weighting or model rules; exclusions; uncertainty rules; and table structure
Stop if
Any material method change is unexplained; comparability is assumed rather than demonstrated; source vintage is ambiguous
Exit
Every material change is documented with its expected effect on comparability and publication language

Retain: Method-change comparison and decision notes

Stage 4reproduction_qa

Analyst

Reproduction and QA

Entry
Method-change review is complete
Required record
Reproduced headline totals; component reconciliations; prior-publication comparison; QA results; opened discrepancy records
Stop if
Headline totals cannot be reproduced; components do not reconcile; material break lacks a discrepancy record
Exit
Required totals reproduce or every material exception has an owned discrepancy record with evidence needs

Retain: QA results and discrepancy log

Stage 5discrepancy_resolution

Analyst + partner lead

Discrepancy and question resolution

Entry
QA has identified questions or confirmed no material discrepancies
Required record
Question/discrepancy log with owner role; materiality; evidence requested; response; decision; disclosure need; and status
Stop if
Material discrepancy remains unresolved; partner response is missing for a decision that depends on it; evidence contradicts proposed interpretation
Exit
Material issues are resolved or explicitly disclosed and any remaining nonmaterial items are documented for handoff

Retain: Resolved question/discrepancy log

Stage 6publication_gate

Designated reviewer + analyst

Publication gate

Entry
Analysis and material discrepancy review are complete
Required record
Final tables; methodology/source notes; required disclosures; citation check; publication handoff; explicit approval or hold record
Stop if
Missing sign-off; stale source or citation; unresolved material issue; release disclosure not carried into public copy
Exit
Designated reviewer records approval or an explicit hold and the public-release decision matches the evidence

Retain: Publication handoff and approval/hold record

Stage 7archive_handoff

Analyst

Archive and next-cycle handoff

Entry
Publication decision is recorded
Required record
Final source package; reviewed tables; methodology version; discrepancy log; approval record; publication URL or hold note; next-cycle open questions
Stop if
Missing provenance; missing approval/hold record; final package cannot reproduce the published or held decision
Exit
Cycle package is archived with enough evidence for a new analyst to reconstruct decisions without relying on oral memory

Retain: Archive manifest and next-cycle handoff note

Product packet

Shared controls, product-specific evidence

The three products remain at a pre-kickoff portfolio position until a real partner cycle is confirmed. Their existing registers supply the source-specific intake, method, and QA requirements.

Pre-kickoff portfolio state

Undocumented Students in U.S. Higher Education

Current position
Current public product and methodology reference reviewed
Cycle trigger
Production kickoff or delivery of a refreshed ACS-based estimate package
Intake evidence
Versioned source extract; data dictionary; modeling/code notes; prior-report methodology; change log; table shells; suppression and uncertainty rules
Method review
Confirm undocumented-identification model, international-student exclusions, DACA-subset logic, ACS vintage, geography, weighting, and any changed eligibility assumptions
QA gate

Reproduce partner national totals; reconcile state tables to documented methods; compare with the prior report; log unexplained breaks; hold publication until material discrepancies are resolved

Next action: At kickoff, confirm partner timeline, exact ACS vintage, method changes, delivery format, review milestones, and final sign-off owner

Coordination record

What a live cycle must leave behind

Intake manifest

Cycle/report identifiers, delivered files, versions, source vintage, methodology package, delivery date, and completeness exceptions.

Method-change record

Prior/current definitions, weighting or model changes, geography, exclusions, uncertainty rules, and expected comparability impact.

Question / discrepancy log

Owner role, materiality, evidence request, response, decision, disclosure need, status, and closure date.

Approval + archive

Final reviewed outputs, methodology version, publication handoff, approval or hold decision, publication URL, and next-cycle open questions.

Operational template

Start a real cycle with a controlled log

The downloadable CSV is intentionally empty. Copy it into the approved working location at kickoff, then validate populated records against the three report IDs, seven stage IDs, controlled record types, materiality values, statuses, and date rules.

Foreign-key controls

Every populated row must reference one of the three recurring report IDs and one of the seven production stage IDs.

Controlled record types

approval, archive, decision, discrepancy, question, stage_evidence

Status discipline

APPROVED, BLOCKED, CLOSED, HELD, OPEN, RESOLVED. Terminal records require a close date; active records cannot carry one.

Public boundary

The blank template is public. Completed partner logs are nonpublic by default and require a separate release decision before disclosure.
