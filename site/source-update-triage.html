R07 · source-change routing

A new file is a review trigger, not automatically a new finding.

Every maintained source has a default route and explicit escalation conditions. Same-method updates can follow routine refresh controls; changes to definitions, denominators, models, geography, privacy rules, or material conclusions move into a higher review path before public interpretation changes.

Decision ladder

Route the kind of change, not merely the publication date.

The first applicable material-change gate controls the review. A routine refresh is available only after higher-order definition and governance questions are cleared.

  1. Metadata onlyURL, title, or date changes update the catalog without manufacturing a research finding.
  2. Same method, new valuesRun the normal refresh, reconciliation, QA, and publication gates.
  3. Method changedDefinition, denominator, model, schema, geography, or reference-period changes stop routine trend use until reviewed.
  4. Question changedNew coverage or a material same-method conclusion shift can open or reprioritize a research question with an explicit decision rule.
  5. Governance changedPrivacy, handling, or release-rule changes are reviewed before collection or publication practice changes.

R07 outcome

Refresh, review methodology, or open a question for a stated reason.

Routine refresh

Use for method-equivalent new IPEDS, benchmark, and recurring-report vintages. Reconciliation and publication QA still apply.

Methodology review

Use when definitions, models, denominators, geography, schema, or timing change. Preserve a vintage break rather than splicing incompatible segments.

Research-question review

Use when new population/geography coverage makes a held question feasible or a material same-method conclusion shift changes the decision need.

Governance review

Use when privacy, handling, or release guidance changes. Do not expand sensitive-data collection by default.
