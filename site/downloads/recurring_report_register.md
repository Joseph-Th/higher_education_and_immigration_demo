# Recurring Research Product Operations Register

This is a portfolio operations model for the three recurring research products named in the target role. It does **not** claim access to, responsibility for, or knowledge of the Presidents' Alliance's internal production schedule. Its purpose is to show how partner-delivered research would be tracked, reviewed, documented, and moved toward publication without inventing deadlines or changing source values.

## Shared production lifecycle

1. **Kickoff and scope lock:** confirm partner contacts, roles, decision owner, delivery format, milestones, current population definition, and release target. Do not infer an annual deadline from a prior publication date.
2. **Versioned intake:** preserve the delivered files, codebook/model notes, prior methodology, change log, table shells, and uncertainty/suppression rules as one dated package before analysis begins.
3. **Method-change review:** compare definitions, source vintage, geography, denominators, weighting/model rules, exclusions, and table structure with the prior published product and the population crosswalk.
4. **Reproduction and QA:** reproduce partner headline totals from the delivered package, reconcile component tables, compare with the prior report, and open a discrepancy log for every unexplained material break.
5. **Analyst review:** separate confirmed findings from unresolved issues; document questions sent back to the partner and the evidence used to close them.
6. **Publication gate:** update methodology/source notes, tables, Portal content, and citations only after material discrepancies are resolved or explicitly disclosed and the designated reviewer/sign-off owner approves the product.
7. **Archive and handoff:** preserve the final source package, reviewed tables, methodology version, discrepancy log, approval record, and publication URL so the next cycle starts from known institutional knowledge.

## Product-specific review notes

### Undocumented Students in U.S. Higher Education

- **Partner context:** American Immigration Council / Presidents' Alliance; confirm current partner roles at production kickoff
- **Schedule rule:** Recurring; production dates and milestones must be confirmed with the partner rather than inferred
- **Monitoring trigger:** Production kickoff or delivery of a refreshed ACS-based estimate package
- **Intake package:** Versioned source extract; data dictionary; modeling/code notes; prior-report methodology; change log; table shells; suppression and uncertainty rules
- **Method-change check:** Confirm undocumented-identification model, international-student exclusions, DACA-subset logic, ACS vintage, geography, weighting, and any changed eligibility assumptions
- **QA gate:** Reproduce partner national totals; reconcile state tables to documented methods; compare with the prior report; log unexplained breaks; hold publication until material discrepancies are resolved
- **Expected publication outputs:** Recurring report tables; methodology notes; Portal data updates; archived source and review package
- **Next action:** At kickoff, confirm partner timeline, exact ACS vintage, method changes, delivery format, review milestones, and final sign-off owner

### Immigrant-Origin Students in Higher Education

- **Partner context:** Migration Policy Institute / Presidents' Alliance; confirm current partner roles at production kickoff
- **Schedule rule:** Recurring; production dates and milestones must be confirmed with the partner rather than inferred
- **Monitoring trigger:** Production kickoff or delivery of a refreshed CPS immigrant-origin analysis package
- **Intake package:** Versioned analytic tables or extract; variable definitions; weighting notes; prior-report methodology; change log; table shells; state-coverage notes
- **Method-change check:** Confirm first- and second-generation definitions, age/education universe, nonimmigrant-student exclusion, CPS vintage, survey weights, state-support rules, and any revised workforce measures
- **QA gate:** Reproduce national headline totals; verify first/second-generation components; compare supported state estimates with prior publication; document sampling/definition changes before narrative use
- **Expected publication outputs:** Recurring report tables; methodology notes; Portal data updates; archived source and review package
- **Next action:** At kickoff, confirm partner timeline, CPS vintage, population-definition changes, state outputs, review milestones, and final sign-off owner

### Undocumented Students Graduating High School

- **Partner context:** Migration Policy Institute / Presidents' Alliance; confirm current partner roles at production kickoff
- **Schedule rule:** Recurring; production dates and milestones must be confirmed with the partner rather than inferred
- **Monitoring trigger:** Production kickoff or delivery of refreshed ACS/graduation-rate inputs
- **Intake package:** Versioned ACS-derived estimates; NCES graduation-rate inputs; model assumptions; prior-report methodology; change log; table shells; demographic/state input tables
- **Method-change check:** Confirm age universe, undocumented-estimation method, pooled ACS period, differential graduation-rate assumptions, state/race-ethnicity inputs, and any revised treatment of recent arrivals
- **QA gate:** Reproduce the annual graduate estimate from documented inputs; trace each assumption; compare demographic/state components with prior report; log sensitivity or definition changes before publication
- **Expected publication outputs:** Recurring report estimates; methodology notes; Portal research update; archived source and review package
- **Next action:** At kickoff, confirm partner timeline, pooled ACS period, NCES rate vintage, model changes, review milestones, and final sign-off owner
