## Resource notes

### IPEDS Complete Data Files

- **Publisher:** NCES
- **Scope:** Institution-level IPEDS downloadable tables and data dictionaries
- **Project use:** Primary source discovery and reproducible collection
- **Status note:** Authoritative access point for complete-data files

### IPEDS Data Release Schedule

- **Publisher:** NCES
- **Scope:** Survey-component publication timing and release sequence
- **Project use:** Source-freshness and next-release monitoring
- **Status note:** Used to distinguish collection timing from publication timing

### Timing of IPEDS Data Collection

- **Publisher:** NCES
- **Scope:** IPEDS collection cycle and reporting-period timing
- **Project use:** Interpret data year collection cycle and release vintage
- **Status note:** Methodological timing reference

### IPEDS Race/Ethnicity and U.S. Nonresident Definitions

- **Publisher:** NCES
- **Scope:** Federal IPEDS reporting definitions for race/ethnicity citizenship and U.S. nonresident classification
- **Project use:** Primary definition source for the population/source crosswalk and public interpretation boundary
- **Status note:** Explicitly distinguishes U.S. nonresidents from DACA undocumented and other eligible noncitizens

### Digest of Education Statistics 2025 Table 306.10

- **Publisher:** NCES
- **Scope:** National fall enrollment at degree-granting postsecondary institutions by selected characteristics and year
- **Project use:** Independent national benchmark and release-vintage audit
- **Status note:** March 2026 publication vintage; 2024 values provisional

### IPEDS Fall Enrollment 2024 Complete Data File

- **Publisher:** NCES
- **Scope:** Institution-level fall enrollment by race/ethnicity gender attendance status and level
- **Project use:** Latest primary enrollment source used by the analysis
- **Status note:** Current project treats Fall 2024 as provisional

### IPEDS Institutional Directory 2024 Complete Data File

- **Publisher:** NCES
- **Scope:** Institution name geography control degree-granting and First Look/Digest universe fields
- **Project use:** Same-year institution metadata and analytic-universe join
- **Status note:** Joined to Fall Enrollment by UNITID

### Higher Ed Immigration Portal National Data

- **Publisher:** Presidents' Alliance / Higher Ed Immigration Portal
- **Scope:** National data for undocumented immigrant-origin and international students in higher education
- **Project use:** Contextual comparison and model for public data presentation; not an input to the IPEDS series
- **Status note:** Current Portal page links to 2026 reports and population-specific data

### Higher Ed Immigration Portal Methodology

- **Publisher:** Presidents' Alliance / Higher Ed Immigration Portal
- **Scope:** Sources and methods used for national and state-level Portal data
- **Project use:** Methodology reference and model for documenting source limitations; not an input to the IPEDS series
- **Status note:** Documents partner data sources and population-specific methodology

### Undocumented Students in U.S. Higher Education 2026

- **Publisher:** Presidents' Alliance / Higher Ed Immigration Portal
- **Scope:** Updated estimates of undocumented students enrolled in U.S. higher education
- **Project use:** Related recurring research product named in the target role; not an input to the IPEDS series
- **Status note:** Posted May 28 2026 with updated national estimates

### Securing America's Talent Pipeline: How Immigrant-Origin Students Power Higher Education

- **Publisher:** Presidents' Alliance / Higher Ed Immigration Portal
- **Scope:** 2023 immigrant-origin student enrollment and higher-education workforce context
- **Project use:** Latest immigrant-origin research product relevant to the target role; not an input to the IPEDS series
- **Status note:** Posted May 5 2026; analysis commissioned from Migration Policy Institute

### A Generation Threatened: Undocumented High School Students Graduate Amidst Uncertain National Climate

- **Publisher:** Presidents' Alliance / Higher Ed Immigration Portal
- **Scope:** Estimates of undocumented students reaching and graduating from U.S. high schools each year
- **Project use:** Latest high-school-graduate research product named in the target role; not an input to the IPEDS series
- **Status note:** Posted February 3 2026; estimates approximately 75000 undocumented graduates annually
