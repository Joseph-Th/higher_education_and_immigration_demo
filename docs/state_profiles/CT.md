# Connecticut (CT) | U.S. Nonresident Enrollment Profile

**Coverage:** Fall 2015-2024, degree-granting institutions in the IPEDS First Look/Digest Title IV universe  
**Latest data status:** Provisional

## Current snapshot

- **15,407** U.S. nonresident students in Fall 2024.
- **7.90%** of total enrollment in the state analytic universe.
- **4,551 undergraduate** and **10,856 graduate** U.S. nonresident students; graduate students account for **70.5%** of the state total.

## Interpretation boundaries

IPEDS U.S. nonresident status is not a complete measure of international, immigrant-origin, undocumented, DACA, or foreign-born students. Counts are Fall Enrollment point-in-time measures. State assignment is based on institution location. Institution membership in the analytic universe can change because of reporting, eligibility, classification, merger, or UNITID changes.
